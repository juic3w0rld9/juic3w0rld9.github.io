Juice WRLD (born Jarad Anthony Higgins) was an American rapper, singer, and songwriter who became a leading figure in the emo rap and SoundCloud rap genres. He rose to mainstream prominence in 2018 with his breakout hit single "Lucid Dreams," which peaked at number two on the US Billboard Hot 100.

Lil Peep (born Gustav Elijah Åhr) was an American rapper, singer, and songwriter who stood at the forefront of the mid-to-late 2010s emo rap scene. By merging elements of pop-punk, emo, indie rock, and trap, he helped define a distinct subgenre that resonated deeply with a new generation of listeners online.

XXXTentacion (born Jahseh Dwayne Ricardo Onfroy, January 23, 1998 – June 18, 2018) was an American rapper, singer, and songwriter who became one of the most prominent and influential figures of the SoundCloud rap era in the late 2010s.
